Lani Wilkinson

Lani Wilkinson is a children’s author and illustrator who believes stories have the power to bring families together and create traditions that last a lifetime.

Living in Hendersonville, Tennessee, with her husband and their four boys, she finds inspiration in everyday moments, family adventures, and the joy of creating.

Lani was inspired to write children’s books as a way to share heartfelt stories while bringing them to life through artwork she loves to create. Her debut book, The Legend of the Great Pumpkin, grew from a cherished tradition passed down through her husband’s family and lovingly adopted by her own. She hopes the story inspires other families to create special traditions and lasting memories together.

When she’s not writing or illustrating, Lani enjoys exploring all kinds of arts and crafts and dreaming up new stories filled with imagination, kindness, and wonder.

Lani looks forward to continuing to write children’s books that families will enjoy reading together for years to come.
